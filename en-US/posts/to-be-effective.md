(Originally written on September 7, 2026)

A good leader I had in the last company I worked in
used to say: "Stop to start and start to finish." I understood
the importance of efectively to harvest the fruits of what I was
doing, and to not get lost in the distractions of new beginnings.

My ex-leader's tip also was related to a
problem called context change. It's necessary spend
some time in certain task to be really engaged
and focused. In case we change from the context of an activity
to the one of another, the work of connecting with what is being
done will need to restart.

All these ideas don't apply only to professional or home
tasks. They also apply to personal goals,
as reading a book or learning a new skill.

At last, but not least, I say that to be effective
is needed to have rhythm. With it, I wanna say isn't efficient
to work when we need rest. And it's not
profitable to rest when we are hounded by the necessity
of the actions life requires. It's needed to rest well to
work well. And is needed to work well to rest well.
